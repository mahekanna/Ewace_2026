## 0. The trading cycle (BR p1 — the outermost loop)

1. **CONTEXT** — context of the market: check global indices (DJIA, S&P500, FTSE, DAX, NIKKEI,
   HSI, Shanghai), DXY, USDINR, bond yields, midcap/smallcap, gold/silver/copper/crude; ratio of
   Midcap/Nifty; leadership-sector relative out-performance; **run scanner & pick stocks**.
2. **SIGNAL** — is there a pattern? → is there a setup using price action + indicator? →
   time cycles, EW, volume profile, options data.
3. **EXECUTION** — 15-min/5-min chart; SL and TGT defined; **risk:reward? YES then execute**
   (worked examples demand ≥ ~1:2 — DECK p68 `157:337`, p69 `500:1200`).
4. For investing use weekly, daily, hourly.

## 1. The chart-analysis steps (D2 p9, verbatim list — the core of the method)

> 1. Start from weekly or daily or hourly or 15 mins.
> 2. **Draw channel.**
> 3. Try to see the pattern → Impulse / Corrective.
> 4. Identify WHICH corrective pattern by **seeing wave b**:
>    - if **b takes LESS time than a** → Diametric, Triangle.
>    - if **b takes MORE time than a** → all correction patterns possible; ZZ or FLAT most likely.
> 5. For counting: start from the **faster retracement**.
> 6. **Plot Ichimoku Cloud.**
> 7. Go to hourly / 15 mins for the trade.

Supporting discipline: top-down degree nesting monthly→weekly→daily→hourly (DECK p56-62);
wave charts plot the high & low of each period; the monowave is the unit; every part of the chart
must carry a label (DECK p44); log/semi-log scale for multi-year counts (DECK p57-58, p80).

## 2. Impulse checklist (DECK p17-19 — 16 essential rules; NW tightening BR p2)

1. 5 segments, 5-3-5-3-5. 2. W2/W4 correct W1/W3. 3. **W2 ≤ 61.8% of W1** (NW; classic EW allows
<100% — BR p2 marks a 62-100% W2 count invalid under NW). 4. W3 never the shortest. 5. **W4 must
not enter W2's territory** (NW; EW says W1's — BR p2), except terminal. 6. Alternation of W2 vs W4
in price, time, severity, intricacy, construction (DECK p28: the small correction sits next to the
extended wave). 7. No part of W1/W3 breaks the **0-2 line**. 8. No part of W3/W5 breaks the
**2-4 line** (except terminal) — Neely confirmation. 9. Fib relationships usually present.
10. Only one of W1/W3/W5 extends. 11. Extended wave ≥ 1.618× the next longest. 12. The two
unextended waves tend to equality. 13. 5th failure possible when 3rd extended. 14. Only 4 of 6
touch-points on the channel — touch-point rule. 15. Post-pattern the **2-4 line must break in ≤
the time of W5** — rule of confirmation. 16. **Time rule: W2 takes ≥ time of W1; W4 ≥ W3**
(D1b p4 marks a 5-day W2 after a 10-day W1 "not valid").

Extensions: usually the 3rd, at 1.618/2.618 of W1 (FIBO); the extended wave visibly subdivides
(D1b p3); W5 = 1.618 of W3 when 3rd extended (BR p2). Terminal impulse (D1 p5, DECK p30, p53):
only in W5/WC, all legs :3, W4 must overlap W1, W3 > W1, time rule waived, then full retrace in
¼-½ of W5's time.

## 3. Corrective classification — measured, via wave B (DECK p32-37, D2, FIBO)

- **Zigzag (5-3-5)**: b < 61.8% of a (can be 1%); c beyond end of a; c ≈ a / 0.618a / 1.618a
  (elongated); **b takes ≥ time of a**; channel it, max 3 of 4 touches.
- **Flat (3-3-5)**: b ≥ 61.8% of a; bands (D2 p3): weak b 61.8-100 / strong (irregular) b >100 /
  running / C-failure / double failure; c = a or 1.382a; b takes more time than a.
- **Triangle (3-3-3-3-3)**: cannot form in wave 2 (except inside terminals, D2 p4); **E smallest**
  (DECK p36); ≥3 legs retrace >50% of prior; **B-D base line must be CLEAN**; other line = A-C if
  C<B else C-E; thrust 100-125% of widest leg; 4 of 6 touches; expanding = spikes at extremes
  (DECK p72); **neutral: C longest with-trend, D longest overall** (DECK p73); **extracting:
  e <<< a and d > b** (D2 p5, DECK p54-55: rallies grow, declines shrink; D always > C).
- **Diametric (7 legs a-g, all :3)**: bow-tie (contract→expand) or diamond; **g≈a, f≈b, e≈c in
  price OR time** (D2 p6, FIBO); time-similar legs (DECK p75 ~20-bar legs; p77/79 repeated ~15-day
  legs).
- **Complex (X waves)**: W-X-Y / W-X-Y-X-Z; **max 2 X waves**; X = any corrective or monowave;
  X < 61.8% of prior pattern (FIBO); **large X ≥ 1.618×W → higher-degree relabel** (D2 p8).
- Impulse-vs-corrective discriminators (DECK p45): corrective takes MORE time than the move it
  corrects (except triangle/terminal/diametric/symmetrical); correctives subdivide visibly;
  **impulses do not fit parallel channels** — a neatly channeled overlapping "trend" is a
  combination (DECK p39-43).
- **Rule of Similarity & Balance**: adjacent same-degree waves within 1/3-3× in price or time
  (DECK p44).

## 4. Confirmation lines — nothing is complete until a line breaks IN TIME (the SOW signature)

| Pattern family | Line to draw | Stage 1 | Stage 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impulse | 2-4 line | break ≤ time of W5 (D1 p4, DECK p19 r15) | retrace to W4 area ≤ same window (D1 p4) |
| Zigzag / Flat | **0-B line** (origin→b) | break ≤ time of C (DECK p33-34; D2 p2 "1st stage ≤ 10 days") | beyond b's extreme (DECK p38; D2 p2 "2nd stage") |
| Triangle | **B-D line** | break ≤ time of E (DECK p37) | thrust 100-125% of widest leg |
| Diametric | boundary after g | break ≤ ~time of g (D2 p6 context) | — |

The 0-2 line conditions the count while it develops (redraw as the correction evolves — DECK p27);
the **"Buy" is marked at the 0-b line break** in the worked mentorship chart (BR p1).
If C ends ON the channel line → expect an x-wave; escalate to complex (DECK p33/35).

## 5. Fib system (FIBO sheet + DECK p14-15, p65)

Roll retracement grids forward swing-by-swing; reactions terminate at 50-61.8%; W3 targets =
extension grid from the W2 low (1.618-2.618 of W1; worked example targets the 2.618-3.0 band,
DECK p65); equality relations as completion signals ((e)=(c) DECK p68; time of III→IV = time of I
DECK p80); Gann horizontal grid for decision levels (DECK p26, p68).

## 6. Time cycles — "Kaal Chakra" (DECK p77-81; deliberately OUT of this repo)

54/108-day Hurst cycles daily (weakness in the **2nd half of the 108-day cycle**), 85w/141w/272w
weekly-monthly; ±10% time leeway; a turn is expected where pattern completion + cycle vertical +
price level coincide (this combination "anticipated Oct 2024", DECK p59). **Repo mapping:** the
"when" layer belongs to chakra_quant via the typed `CycleSignal` seam
(`ewave.signals.cycle_seam`) — the 7th confluence strand. Not re-implemented here by design.

## 7. Ichimoku Cloud (DECK p49-51) and execution

Settings 9/26/52, displacement 26. **Trade filter: Tenkan & Kijun moving together in the trade
direction AND price above (long) / below (short) the cloud**; pullbacks to the lines while the
cloud relationship holds are re-entries (Titan 3× SELL, DECK p51). Execution drops to 15m/5m
("5 mins Ichimoku", BR p2) with SL at the pattern invalidation, target at Gann/extension/equality/
channel level, and **R:R ≥ ~1:2 written down before entry** (DECK p68-69).
