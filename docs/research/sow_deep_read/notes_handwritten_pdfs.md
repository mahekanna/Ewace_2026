## D1 p5: Terminal Impulse (Ending Diagonal)
- 3-3-3-3-3; can form ONLY in W5 or WC; W2 CAN retrace more than 61.8% of W1; W4 SHOULD enter W1 (overlap);
  W3 should go above W1; time rule NOT necessary here (W2 can take < time than W1; W4 vs W3 same).
- Post-pattern: retraced in 1/2 or 1/4 of the TIME of W5 (annotation on the reversal arrow).

## D2 p4: Triangle (3-3-3-3-3 a-b-c-d-e)

## D2 p7: Complex corrections (X wave)

## D2 p9: *** STEPS (the method, verbatim list) ***
1. Start from weekly or daily or hourly or 15 mins.
2. Draw channel.
3. Try to see the pattern -> Impulse / Corrective.
4. Identify WHICH corrective pattern by SEEING WAVE B:
   - if b takes LESS time than a -> Diametric, Triangle.
   - if b takes MORE time than a -> all correction patterns possible; chances of ZZ or FLAT high.
5. For counting: start from the FASTER RETRACEMENT.
6. Plot Ichimoku Cloud.
7. Go to hourly / 15 mins for the trade.

## FIBO p1: Fibo system (per pattern)
- Impulse: usually 3rd extended = 1.618 or 2.618 of W1; W5 & W1 tend to equality; W2 NOT MORE THAN 61.8% of W1;
  W4 & W2 can relate by Fibo.
- Zigzag: b <= 61.8% of a RULE; c ≈ a, c = 61.8% of a, c = 1.618 × a.
- FLAT: b >= 61.8% of a; b = a or b ≈ 138.2% of a.
- Triangle: 3 legs 50% Fibo with prior leg.
- Diametric: g ≈ a or g ≈ 61.8% of a; f ≈ b price or time; e ≈ c price or time.
- X: less than 61.8% of prior pattern.
