## p17: ESSENTIAL RULES FOR AN IMPULSE (1-5)
- RULES:
  1. Internally comprises 5 segments/waves, structured 5-3-5-3-5. Three waves thrust in impulse direction (1st, 3rd & 5th).
  2. 2nd & 4th waves correct the 1st & 3rd waves, respectively.
  3. End point of Wave 2 cannot retrace more than 61.8% of wave 1.
  4. 3rd cannot be "the" shortest of the 1st, 3rd & 5th waves.
  5. 4th wave should not enter the area covered by 2nd (except in terminal impulse) — Rule of overlap.
- Diagram: two "3rd Wave Extension" schematics with NeoWave-style structure labels (1:5, 2:3, x3:5, 4:3, 5:5) and parallel channel lines; extended wave marked with "x".

## p18: ESSENTIAL RULES FOR AN IMPULSE (6-10)
- RULES:
  6. 2nd & 4th wave should alternate in price, time, severity, intricacy, construction — Rule of Alternation.
  7. No part of 1st or 3rd should break the 0-2 line.
  8. No part of 3rd or 5th should break the 2-4 line (except in terminal impulse) — Neely's Confirmation Rule.
  9. Internal & external Fibonacci relationships are usually present among the segments.
  10. Only one among the 1st, 3rd or 5th waves can extend — Extension rule.
- METHOD/PROCESS: implies drawing two specific trendlines on every suspected impulse: the 0-2 line and the 2-4 line, and using violations as validation/invalidation tests.

## p19: ESSENTIAL RULES FOR AN IMPULSE (11-16)
- RULES:
  11. The extended wave should be at least 1.618 of the next longest — Extension rule.
  12. The two unextended waves tend towards equality — Rule of Equality.
  13. 5th failure possible, especially when 3rd is already an extended wave.
  14. Only 4 (out of possible 6) touch-points should touch the two opposing trend lines — Neely's Touch point Rule.
  15. 2-4 line should be cut in lesser or equal time period compared to 5th — Neely's Rule of Confirmation (post-pattern break of the 2-4 line must take ≤ the time wave 5 took).
  16. Corrective wave should take more time than preceding Impulse: Wave 2 should take same or more time than wave 1; Wave 4 should take same or more time than wave 3. (NeoWave time rule.)

## p27: CHANELLING TECHNIQUE
- RULES/METHOD (Neely diagrams):
  - Draw the 0-2 trendline from pattern origin (0) through end of wave 2. If wave 2 is a running/complex correction (diagram shows (1)-a-b-x-a-b-c-(2)), the ORIGINAL 0-2 line must be ADAPTED/REVISED — redraw so no price crosses it ("Original 0-2" vs "Adapted 0-2"; "Revised Trendline from diagram A").
  - Right diagrams ("Correct"): after wave 3 begins, the 0-2 trendline's break tells you wave 2 is finished; if price breaks the original trendline then makes a new low, REVISE the trendline to the new touch points ("Original Trendline" vs "Revised Trendline") before trusting the next break. Arrows show the breakout direction test at the 0-2 line.
- METHOD/PROCESS: channeling = an explicit stepwise procedure: draw 0-2 → adapt as correction evolves → the decisive signal is the clean break of the correctly-drawn trendline (start of wave 3).

## SYNTHESIS p1-31

### Analysis sequence taught in these pages
1. Start on the LARGEST timeframe on log/semi-log scale (Sensex monthly since 1970s-80s, p21-22): establish the super-cycle count first, then work down (weekly Nifty p20 → daily p23-25 → 1-hour p26). Every lower-timeframe count must nest inside the higher one.
2. Classify every swing as impulse ("5") or correction ("3") (p16, p31). Impulses must pass the full 16-rule checklist (p17-19); if legs are all ":3"s with overlap, label it terminal (p30); 7-legged a-b-c-d-e-f-g structures = NeoWave diametric (p20, p21, p25).
3. Draw the structural trendlines immediately: the 0-2 line and the 2-4 line (p18 rules 7-8), adapting/revising them as the correction evolves (p27 channeling technique). Trendline breaks — not labels — are the confirmation triggers (Neely's confirmation rules 8 & 15).
4. Apply Fibonacci retracement grids to each completed swing and roll them forward (p14-15): expect wave-2-type reactions and bear-market bounces to terminate at 50-61.8%; the 61.8% of the whole prior advance is the maximal-degree support.
5. Check alternation between waves 2 and 4 in price, time, severity, intricacy, construction (p18 rule 6, p28) — use the size of wave 2 to anticipate which wave extends (small 2 → big 4 and/or extended 5, etc.).
6. Overlay TIME: fixed-length time-cycle verticals (84 weeks on weekly Nifty p20; 272 weeks on Sensex p21-22; multiple daily cycles simultaneously p25) plus time-equality relationships (Time III→IV = Time I, p22; wave 2 time ≥ wave 1 time, wave 4 ≥ wave 3, rule 16 p19). A turn is expected where pattern completion, fib/Gann price level, and cycle line coincide.
7. Fix price targets with measured moves and written levels: equal-box projections (61000 Sensex p22), terminal targets (92000/108000 p21), Gann horizontals (23486, 22877 p26), and leg-equality checks ((e)=(c), p26).
8. Keep dual counts alive on the chart ("(A) or (1)", "(B) or (2)", p23-24) until price resolves them; project the expected 3-4-5 roadmap ahead, then revisit and relabel as it completes.
9. Ichimoku Cloud is the final confirmation layer (course item 7, p10) — not yet detailed in p1-31.

### 10 most operationally specific rules found (p1-31)
1. Wave 2 cannot retrace more than 61.8% of wave 1 (p17 rule 3).
2. Wave 3 cannot be the shortest of waves 1, 3, 5 (p17 rule 4).
3. Wave 4 must not enter wave 2's price territory except in a terminal impulse — rule of overlap (p17 rule 5).
4. No part of wave 1 or 3 may break the 0-2 line; no part of wave 3 or 5 may break the 2-4 line (except terminal) — Neely confirmation (p18 rules 7-8).
5. The extended wave must be at least 1.618x the next-longest wave; only one of 1/3/5 may extend; the two unextended waves tend to equality (p18 rule 10, p19 rules 11-12).
6. Only 4 of the possible 6 touch-points may touch the two channel lines — Neely touch-point rule (p19 rule 14).
7. After the impulse, the 2-4 line must be broken in less than or equal time than wave 5 took to form — Neely's rule of confirmation (p19 rule 15).
8. Time rule: wave 2 takes same or more time than wave 1; wave 4 same or more than wave 3; corrections take more time than the preceding impulse (p19 rule 16).
9. Counter-trend bounces and wave-2 reactions terminate at the 50%-61.8% retracement; a full bear market can reach 61.8% of the entire prior multi-year advance (p14-15 worked Sensex examples).
10. Turn timing: fixed time cycles (84-week weekly, 272-week monthly, multiple daily cycles) drawn as verticals — expect reversals where a completed wave structure meets a cycle line, refined by time-equality (e.g. Time of III→IV = Time of I) and leg price-equality (e.g. (e)=(c)) plus Gann/round-number levels (24103, 23486, 22877).
