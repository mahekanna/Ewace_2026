## p72: "EXPANDING TRIANGLE (SPIKES AT EXTREMES)" — pattern reference
- RULES:
  - Expanding triangle: upper boundary rising, lower boundary falling (diverging lines).
  - Signature stated in title and diagram: SPIKES occur AT THE EXTREMES — the touches of the expanding boundaries are quick spikes ("Spike High" marked twice on the upper line, "Spike Low" on the lower line), not sustained travel; price pierces/tags the line and reverses sharply.
- METHOD/PROCESS: identify an expanding formation by drawing the two diverging lines across the spike extremes; expect each boundary touch to be a spike-and-reverse, later touches exceeding earlier ones.

## p73: Neutral Triangle — pattern reference (NeoWave)
- RULES (text on slide, verbatim): "Wave-C is the longest wave in the direction of the trend. Wave-d is the longest wave against the trend is usually the longest wave of the entire pattern."
  - Diagram: A-B-C-D-E triangle whose boundary lines are roughly parallel/slightly diverging (neither clearly contracting nor expanding) — neutral triangle.
  - C is the longest with-trend leg; D (against trend) is usually the longest leg of the whole pattern.
  - Caption under diagram: "During this period, the trend is down" — the neutral triangle shown forms as a consolidation within a larger downtrend (black dots mark the pattern's start after a top and its end at E before continuation).
- METHOD/PROCESS: bracket the triangle between the two black dots (start after the preceding impulse peak, end at E); draw the B-D upper line and A-C-E lower line; classify contracting vs expanding vs neutral by whether the lines converge, diverge, or run parallel.

## p75: "Diametric Formation" — pattern reference (NeoWave)
- RULES:
  - Diametric = 7-legged pattern labeled A, B, C, D, E, F, G.
  - Shape drawn: first half CONTRACTS (upper line down through A–C, lower line up through B–D converge around D), second half EXPANDS (lines diverge from D out through E/F to G) — the classic "bowtie/diamond" outline.

- METHOD/PROCESS: draw A–C and B–D lines for the contracting half; after D, draw the diverging E–G and D–F lines; the pattern completes at G, after which the prior trend resumes (chart continues up after G). X-axis shown in bars (1…141) — leg time similarity across ~20-bar increments.

## SYNTHESIS p63-92

### The analysis sequence taught in these pages
1. **Start on the higher timeframe (weekly/daily) and classify the big structure first** — decide impulse vs corrective. Heavy overlap + similar-sized legs ⇒ count as a NeoWave corrective: triangle (contracting/expanding/neutral), 7-legged diametric (a–g), or combinations joined by x-waves (p64 USDINR, p71 ICICI, p76 JSW).
2. **Draw the structural lines that ARE the count**: channel lines around the whole move; pattern boundary lines (B-D vs A-C-E for triangles; contracting-then-expanding "bowtie" for diametrics); the NeoWave **0-b trendline** from the correction's origin through wave-b (p63) — line breaks/tags confirm leg completion.
3. **Label legs with time counts** (bars/days per leg, e.g. repeated ~15-day legs on Nifty p77/p79) — time similarity confirms corrective legs of the same pattern.
4. **Drop to the hourly for the trade**: locate the terminal leg (e of a triangle, g of a diametric, c of a wave-4 zigzag), often at a channel boundary or GANN level (p66–70).

### 10 most operationally specific rules found (p63–92)
1. Draw the **0-b trendline** (origin of correction through wave-b) — its break confirms the corrective structure's direction/completion (p63).
2. **Neutral triangle**: wave-C is the longest with-trend wave; wave-D (against trend) is usually the longest wave of the entire pattern (p73, verbatim).
3. **Expanding triangle**: boundary touches occur as **spikes at the extremes** — quick pierce-and-reverse at diverging lines (p72, p74).
4. **Diametric** = 7 legs a–g, contracting first half then expanding second half; the move completes only at g — after e, still expect f (bounce to broken line) and terminal g before reversal (p75, p67 DLF: f to ~700 then g to ~575; p70 L&T: reversal only after g; p76 JSW: g = wave 4 end).
5. **Wave-3 target = 2.618–3.0 Fibonacci extension** of wave 1 projected from the wave-2 low (Bajaj Finance: 8,743.65–9,030 from 1=7,530.90/0=6,731); wave (iv)/4 holds near the 1.618–2.0 levels (p65).
6. **Equality relations as targets**: (e)=(c) inside triangles (p68); Time of Wave III→IV = time of Wave I (p80).
7. **Minimum ~1:2 risk-reward stated on-chart before entry**: Nifty short risk 157 / reward 337 pts, invalidated above 22921, target 22427; Bank Nifty short risk 500 / reward 1200 pts, target 47783 with SL above the b/v rebound high (p68–69).
